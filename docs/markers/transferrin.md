# Transferrin (`transferrin`)

Iron-transport protein made in the liver; its serum concentration is the basis of total iron-binding capacity. It is a negative acute-phase protein, so it falls in inflammation, malnutrition and liver disease, and rises in iron deficiency and pregnancy.

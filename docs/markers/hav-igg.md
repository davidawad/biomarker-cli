# Hepatitis A Antibody IgG (`hav-igg`)

IgG antibody to hepatitis A virus (anti-HAV IgG). Positive after past infection or hepatitis A vaccination, so it shows immunity; it is most often checked after vaccination to confirm a response. Many laboratories report it qualitatively (positive or negative); quantitative assays report mIU/mL. Expected result: positive (immune) after infection or vaccination; negative in people without immunity.

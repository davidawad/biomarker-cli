# Anti-Parietal Cell Antibody (`anti-parietal-cell`)

Serum autoantibody against the gastric parietal cell H+/K+ ATPase, detected by indirect immunofluorescence (IIF, reported as a titer) or by immunodot or ELISA (reported as positive or negative). Used to screen for autoimmune atrophic gastritis and pernicious anemia. Positive results are non-specific: they also occur in other autoimmune disease and in a minority of healthy people. Expected result: negative.

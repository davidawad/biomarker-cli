# Specific IgE, Cat Dander (`ige-cat-dander`)

Serum IgE specific to cat dander allergen, used to support the diagnosis of IgE-mediated cat allergy. Interpret with symptoms and skin testing, since a positive level does not by itself prove clinical allergy.

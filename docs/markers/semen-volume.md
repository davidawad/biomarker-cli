# Semen Volume (`semen-volume`)

Volume of one ejaculate, measured in a semen analysis. Low volume can indicate incomplete collection, obstruction or ejaculatory or accessory gland problems; it is one of the parameters used to judge male fertility potential. The WHO 2010 manual gives a one-sided lower reference limit of 1.5 mL for fertile men.

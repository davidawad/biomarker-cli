# Homocysteine (`homocysteine`)

Total homocysteine (tHcy) is a sulfur amino acid whose blood level rises with low folate, vitamin B12 or B6 status and with reduced kidney function. Raised values (hyperhomocysteinaemia) are a metabolic marker of B-vitamin insufficiency and are associated with vascular disease risk.

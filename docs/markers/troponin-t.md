# Troponin T (`troponin-t`)

Cardiac troponin T, a myocardial injury marker measured in blood. The ranges below are age- and sex-specific cut-offs for the 99th percentile upper reference limit of the Roche high-sensitivity cardiac troponin T (hs-cTnT) assay, as proposed from three population cohorts; limits are assay-specific and do not transfer to other troponin methods.

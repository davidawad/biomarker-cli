# Aldosterone/Renin Ratio (direct renin concentration) (`arr-direct-renin`)

Aldosterone (ng/dL) divided by direct renin concentration (mIU/L). Screening ratio for primary aldosteronism when renin is measured as a mass or activity-independent concentration rather than plasma renin activity.

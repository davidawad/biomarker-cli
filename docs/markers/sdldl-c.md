# Small Dense LDL Cholesterol (`sdldl-c`)

Cholesterol carried in small, dense LDL particles, measured directly with a homogeneous assay (Denka Seiken). Its concentration can be high even when LDL-C is at goal, and it predicts coronary heart disease risk independently of LDL-C.

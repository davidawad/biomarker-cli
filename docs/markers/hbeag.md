# Hepatitis B e Antigen (`hbeag`)

Hepatitis B e antigen (HBeAg), a serum marker associated with active hepatitis B viral replication and infectivity. Tracked in people with chronic hepatitis B to guide monitoring and treatment decisions. Expected result: negative in most people without active replication; positive indicates active replication.

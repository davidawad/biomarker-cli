# Urine Protein (`urine-protein`)

Total protein concentration in a random urine sample, often reported from a dipstick as negative/trace, 30, 100 or 300 mg/dL. Persistent proteinuria is a marker of kidney damage. The range below is a dipstick finding from one study; guideline thresholds (KDIGO) are expressed as ratios to creatinine or 24-hour excretion and are covered by urine-pcr and urine-protein-24h.

# Soluble ST2 (`st2`)

Soluble isoform of suppression of tumorigenicity 2, released by cardiomyocytes and fibroblasts under mechanical strain; measured as a prognostic marker in heart failure and cardiovascular risk. Expected result in healthy people: within the reference interval, which is lower in females and varies with age in males.

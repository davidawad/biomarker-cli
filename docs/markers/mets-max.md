# Peak METs (`mets-max`)

Peak metabolic equivalents achieved on a symptom-limited exercise treadmill test (cardiorespiratory fitness). Lower peak METs are associated with higher mortality and major adverse cardiovascular event (MACE) risk in cohort studies.

# Anti-Cyclic Citrullinated Peptide Antibodies (`anti-ccp`)

Antibodies against citrullinated protein antigens (ACPA). They are highly specific for rheumatoid arthritis and are used in diagnosis and classification, often together with rheumatoid factor.

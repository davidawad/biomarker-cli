# Reticulocytes (absolute) (`reticulocytes-abs`)

Absolute number of reticulocytes per litre of blood, the young red cells released from the marrow, as counted by automated analysers. Low counts suggest reduced marrow output; high counts indicate increased erythropoiesis, as after blood loss or haemolysis.

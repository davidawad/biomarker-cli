# Neutrophil-to-lymphocyte ratio (NLR) (`nlr`)

Absolute neutrophil count divided by absolute lymphocyte count, a simple calculated index of systemic inflammation. Higher values are associated with infection, inflammation, stress and worse outcomes in cardiovascular disease, infection and cancer; it is not diagnostic on its own.

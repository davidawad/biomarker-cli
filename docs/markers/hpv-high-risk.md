# HPV High-Risk Genotypes DNA (Cervical) (`hpv-high-risk`)

Nucleic acid test that detects DNA of high-risk human papillomavirus genotypes in a cervical specimen, reported as positive or negative for high-risk HPV, and often with separate results for genotype 16 and 18. It is used in cervical cancer screening and for triage of abnormal cytology. Expected result: negative (not detected).

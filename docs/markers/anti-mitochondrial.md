# Anti-Mitochondrial Antibody (AMA, M2) (`anti-mitochondrial`)

Serum autoantibody to mitochondrial antigens, mainly the M2 (E2 subunit) antigens of the 2-oxo-acid dehydrogenase complexes. Detected by indirect immunofluorescence (IIF, reported as a titer) or by M2-specific assays. Tracked as the principal serologic marker of primary biliary cholangitis (PBC). Expected result: negative. A positive result is diagnostic only together with cholestatic liver tests and liver histology.

# Bilirubin, Transcutaneous (Newborn) (`bilirubin-transcutaneous`)

Skin bilirubin estimated non-invasively with a transcutaneous bilirubinometer (TcB) on the newborn's skin, used to screen for neonatal hyperbilirubinemia and decide whether a serum total bilirubin (TSB) is needed. TcB underestimates TSB, most in phototherapy. Expected result: below the age-specific phototherapy threshold; a TcB above the cut-off below means a serum TSB should be measured.

# Creatine Kinase-MB (`ck-mb`)

Mass concentration of the MB isoenzyme of creatine kinase, a heart-enriched enzyme measured to detect myocardial injury. Expected result in healthy people: at or below the age- and sex-specific upper reference limit. Infant limits (under 1 year) from the same study are not stored because whole-year age keys cannot express them.

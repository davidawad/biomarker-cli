# Mercury, Urine (`mercury-urine`)

Urinary mercury, reflecting inorganic mercury exposure from dental amalgam, and some exposure from diet and occupation. Tracked for exposure assessment; the typical result in an unexposed person is low. Creatinine-corrected values are reported in µg/g creatinine in the cited studies.

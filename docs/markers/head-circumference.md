# Head Circumference (`head-circumference`)

Occipital-frontal head circumference, a growth measurement. Microcephaly is defined from age- and sex-specific growth standards (WHO Child Growth Standards z-scores); the ranges below give the term-newborn centimetre equivalents of the WHO -2 SD and -3 SD cut-offs as listed in Pan American Health Organization and WHO case definitions.

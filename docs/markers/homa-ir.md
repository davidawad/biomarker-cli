# HOMA-IR (`homa-ir`)

Homeostasis model assessment of insulin resistance, calculated from fasting glucose and fasting insulin. Population-specific cut-offs and reference intervals differ by assay formula (HOMA1 vs HOMA2), sex, menopausal status and age; the ranges below are population-derived, not universal diagnostic thresholds.

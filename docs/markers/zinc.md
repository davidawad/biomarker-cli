# Zinc, Serum (`zinc`)

Serum or plasma zinc, the usual laboratory measure of zinc status. Low values indicate zinc deficiency at the population level, and the IZiNCG cut-offs depend on age, sex and fasting status of the blood sample. Cut-offs are population-level tools and are not diagnostic for an individual.

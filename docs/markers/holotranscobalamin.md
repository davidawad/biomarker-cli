# Holotranscobalamin (`holotranscobalamin`)

Holotranscobalamin (holoTC, also called active B12) is the fraction of serum B12 bound to transcobalamin, the form that cells can take up. Low values suggest that less B12 is available to tissues, and it is proposed as an earlier marker than total B12, although it is less established and assay-dependent.

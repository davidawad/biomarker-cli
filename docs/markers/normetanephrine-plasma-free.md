# Normetanephrine, Plasma Free (`normetanephrine-plasma-free`)

Free normetanephrine, the O-methylated metabolite of norepinephrine, measured in plasma. Elevated values support a diagnosis of pheochromocytoma or paraganglioma; it is also the most sensitive plasma marker for many of these tumours.

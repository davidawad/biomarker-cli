# 5-Hydroxyindoleacetic Acid (5-HIAA), Urine (`urine-5-hiaa`)

Serotonin metabolite excreted in urine, usually collected over 24 hours; the standard biochemical test for carcinoid syndrome and serotonin-secreting neuroendocrine tumours, also used to monitor treatment. Expected result: at or below the upper limit of normal in the first range below (50 umol/24h); values above it are elevated. Ranges are in the umol/24h unit the source states (companion unit).

# Anti-Glomerular Basement Membrane Antibody (`anti-gbm`)

IgG autoantibody against the glomerular basement membrane (anti-GBM, collagen IV alpha-3 NC1 domain), measured by a chemiluminescence immunoassay and reported in vendor chemiluminescence units (CU), which are not interchangeable with U/mL from other assays. Diagnostic for Goodpasture syndrome (anti-GBM disease). Expected result: negative (not detected).

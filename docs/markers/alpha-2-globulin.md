# Alpha-2 Globulin (SPEP) (`alpha-2-globulin`)

Alpha-2 globulin fraction of serum protein electrophoresis (SPEP), reported as g/dL of serum. It includes haptoglobin, alpha-2 macroglobulin and ceruloplasmin, so it rises in inflammation and nephrotic-range protein loss and can fall in liver disease. People track it when SPEP is ordered for protein abnormalities or inflammation. Expected result: within the reference interval for the patient's sex (about 0.60-1.10 g/dL combined in the cited adult study).

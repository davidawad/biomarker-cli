# Urine Glucose (`urine-glucose`)

Glucose in urine (glucosuria), measured by semiquantitative dipstick or a laboratory hexokinase method. Glucose normally appears in urine only when blood glucose exceeds the renal threshold, or with renal glucosuria or SGLT2 inhibitor use. The range below is the study case definition for glucosuria cited; no guideline reference interval was verified.

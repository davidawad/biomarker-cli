# Eicosapentaenoic acid (EPA) (`epa`)

Eicosapentaenoic acid (20:5n-3), a long-chain omega-3 fatty acid from marine foods, reported as a percent of total fatty acids in the blood lipid fraction measured. Low values indicate low marine omega-3 intake or status.

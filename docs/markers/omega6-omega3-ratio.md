# Omega-6/Omega-3 Ratio (`omega6-omega3-ratio`)

Ratio of omega-6 to omega-3 polyunsaturated fatty acids in plasma or red cells, computed from the measured fatty-acid totals. Tracked as an index of dietary fatty-acid balance. Expected result: a number with no single target; lower values are generally considered more favourable. Higher ratios were associated with higher all-cause and cardiovascular mortality in one UK Biobank cohort (PMID 38578269), and a 2008 review states the optimal ratio varies by disease (PMID 18408140).

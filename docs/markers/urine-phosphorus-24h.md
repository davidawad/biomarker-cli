# Urine Phosphorus, 24 Hour (`urine-phosphorus-24h`)

Total phosphorus excreted in urine over 24 hours (mg/day). 24-hour excretion companion of the spot-concentration marker urine-phosphorus. Varies with diet; used in kidney stone evaluation.

# Vanillylmandelic Acid (VMA), Urine (`urine-vma`)

Urinary vanillylmandelic acid (VMA), a catecholamine (epinephrine and norepinephrine) metabolite. Measured to screen for and monitor catecholamine-secreting tumors, mainly neuroblastoma in children and pheochromocytoma in adults. Expected result: at or below the age-specific upper reference limit. Values are creatinine-adjusted and are higher in infants; interfering medications and diet can raise results.

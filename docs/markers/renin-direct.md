# Direct Renin Concentration (`renin-direct`)

Concentration of active renin in plasma measured directly by immunoassay, without the generation step used for plasma renin activity. Used with aldosterone to calculate the aldosterone-to-renin ratio when PRA is unavailable, and to judge renin suppression in primary aldosteronism.

# Rheumatoid Factor (`rheumatoid-factor`)

Autoantibody (mostly IgM) against the Fc portion of IgG. Positive results support a diagnosis of rheumatoid arthritis and feed into classification scores; low titres are also seen in other conditions and in healthy older adults.

# Hepatitis B Virus DNA (`hbv-dna`)

Amount of hepatitis B virus DNA in serum. It is used to decide whether chronic hepatitis B needs antiviral treatment and, in pregnancy, whether tenofovir should be given to prevent transmission to the baby.

# Citrate, Urine (`urine-citrate`)

Citrate excreted in urine, usually collected over 24 hours. Low excretion (hypocitraturia) is a major, treatable risk factor for calcium kidney stones. Expected result: at or above the cut-offs in the ranges below; values below the adult definition indicate hypocitraturia. Pediatric cut-offs are normalised to body surface area and are a companion unit.

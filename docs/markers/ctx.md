# C-Terminal Telopeptide (β-CrossLaps, serum CTX) (`ctx`)

Serum fragment of type I collagen released during bone resorption (beta-CrossLaps, CTX-I). A bone resorption marker used to assess fracture risk and response to antiresorptive treatment. Sample handling matters: measure in the morning after an overnight fast.

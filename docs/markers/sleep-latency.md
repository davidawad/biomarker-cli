# Sleep Latency (`sleep-latency`)

Time from lights-out or going to bed until the onset of sleep, in minutes. Tracked to assess how quickly a person falls asleep and as one of the criteria for insomnia. Expected result for most adults is a short latency; a usual or average latency of 20 minutes or longer is the research cut-off used to classify primary insomnia, so values under that are below it.

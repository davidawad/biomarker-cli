# Beta-2 Glycoprotein I IgM Antibody (`b2gp1-igm`)

IgM autoantibody against beta-2 glycoprotein I, measured by solid-phase immunoassay (ELISA, chemiluminescence, or fluorescence enzyme immunoassay). Used as a laboratory criterion for antiphospholipid syndrome, alongside anticardiolipin and lupus anticoagulant testing. Results are assay-specific and reported as units against an assay cut-off. Expected result: negative (below the assay cut-off).

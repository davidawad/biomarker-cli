# Inhibin A, Maternal Serum (`inhibin-a`)

Placental and ovarian peptide measured in maternal serum in the second trimester, expressed as a multiple of the median (MoM). It is part of the quadruple Down syndrome screen. Raised values are associated with pre-eclampsia and with small-for-gestational-age infants.

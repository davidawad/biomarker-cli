# Visceral Adipose Tissue (`visceral-adipose-tissue`)

Visceral adipose tissue (VAT) mass, the fat stored in the abdominal cavity around organs, estimated by whole-body DXA. People track it as a cardiometabolic risk marker; VAT is associated with blood pressure, triglycerides, LDL-cholesterol, glucose and insulin resistance. Expected result: sex-specific; roughly twice as high in men as in women in young adults. Reference values below come from one healthy 20-30 year-old sample; no clinical cut-off for DXA-derived VAT is established here.

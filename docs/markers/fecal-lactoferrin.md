# Fecal Lactoferrin (`fecal-lactoferrin`)

Lactoferrin measured in stool by immunoassay as a marker of intestinal inflammation, used to help distinguish inflammatory bowel disease from irritable bowel syndrome. Expected result: below the cut-off used in the cited study, i.e. not indicative of intestinal inflammation. The ranges below are single-study cut-offs, not reference intervals.

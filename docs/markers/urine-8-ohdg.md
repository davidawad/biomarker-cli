# 8-Hydroxy-2'-Deoxyguanosine (8-OHdG), Urine (`urine-8-ohdg`)

Urinary 8-hydroxy-2'-deoxyguanosine (8-OHdG; also reported as 8-oxo-7,8-dihydro-2'-deoxyguanosine, 8-oxodG), a marker of oxidative damage to DNA. People track it as a non-invasive marker of oxidative stress. Spot urine results are normalized to creatinine. The expected result is a numeric level; the cited sources give population reference intervals only.

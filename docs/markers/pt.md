# Prothrombin time (`pt`)

Clotting time of citrated plasma after tissue factor and calcium are added, reflecting the extrinsic and common pathways. Prolonged values suggest factor VII, X, V, II or fibrinogen deficiency, vitamin K antagonist effect or liver disease; it is standardised as INR for anticoagulation monitoring.

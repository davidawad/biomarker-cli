# Activated partial thromboplastin time (`aptt`)

Clotting time of citrated plasma after a contact activator and calcium are added, reflecting the intrinsic and common pathways. Prolonged values suggest heparin effect, factor VIII, IX or XI deficiency, lupus anticoagulant or other factor deficits; it is the usual monitor of unfractionated heparin.

# Hepatitis A Antibody IgM (`hav-igm`)

IgM antibody to hepatitis A virus (anti-HAV IgM), which indicates recent or acute hepatitis A infection. Tracked to diagnose acute hepatitis A in people with liver symptoms or known exposure. Expected result: negative (not detected) unless there is current or recent infection.

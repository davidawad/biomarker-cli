# Hepatitis C Virus RNA (`hcv-rna`)

Amount of hepatitis C virus RNA in blood. Used to diagnose active infection and to confirm cure: undetectable HCV RNA about 12 weeks after completing treatment is sustained virologic response (SVR), which is treated as virologic cure.

# Helicobacter pylori Urea Breath Test (`h-pylori-breath`)

Non-invasive test for active Helicobacter pylori infection. The patient drinks a labelled urea substrate (13C or 14C) and exhaled breath is analysed for labelled CO2; the result is the change from baseline (delta over baseline, DOB) in per mil. Reported as positive or negative against a cut-off. Expected result: negative (DOB below the cut-off).

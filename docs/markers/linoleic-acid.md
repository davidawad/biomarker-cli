# Linoleic acid (LA) (`linoleic-acid`)

Linoleic acid (18:2n-6), the main dietary omega-6 polyunsaturated fatty acid, reported as a percent of total fatty acids in the blood lipid fraction measured. It reflects intake of vegetable oils; very low values suggest low essential fatty acid status.

# Phylloquinone (vitamin K1) (`vitamin-k1`)

Plasma phylloquinone (vitamin K1), the dietary form of vitamin K used for clotting and bone/vascular carboxylation. Low values reflect low intake or fat malabsorption and are used to assess vitamin K status, mainly in lipid malabsorption; values are very low and assay-dependent.

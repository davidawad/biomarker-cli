# Copeptin (`copeptin`)

C-terminal fragment of the vasopressin precursor, released in equimolar amounts with arginine vasopressin (AVP) and far more stable to measure. Used as a surrogate for AVP in the work-up of polyuria: basal, hypertonic saline-stimulated and arginine-stimulated levels help separate AVP deficiency (central diabetes insipidus), nephrogenic diabetes insipidus and primary polydipsia.

# Mean platelet volume (`mpv`)

Average volume of circulating platelets, an automated analyser index. Larger platelets are younger and more reactive; high values are associated with increased platelet turnover and cardiovascular risk, and low values with marrow failure. It is analyser-dependent and should be interpreted with the platelet count.

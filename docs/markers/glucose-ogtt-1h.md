# Glucose, 1-hour OGTT (75 g) (`glucose-ogtt-1h`)

Plasma glucose 1 hour after a 75 g oral glucose load. Used for gestational diabetes screening (IADPSG) and, per the 2024 IDF position statement, to detect intermediate hyperglycaemia and type 2 diabetes.

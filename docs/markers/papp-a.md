# Pregnancy-Associated Plasma Protein A (`papp-a`)

Placenta-derived protein measured in maternal serum in the first trimester, expressed as a multiple of the median (MoM) for gestational age and maternal weight. Low values are associated with aneuploidy and with later placenta-related complications such as pre-eclampsia, growth restriction and preterm birth.

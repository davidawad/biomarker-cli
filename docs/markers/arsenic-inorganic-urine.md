# Arsenic, Inorganic Urine (`arsenic-inorganic-urine`)

Urinary inorganic arsenic exposure marker, measured as the sum of inorganic arsenic and its methylated metabolites (iAs + MMA + DMA). Tracked for exposure from contaminated drinking water, food and occupation. Expected result in a non-exposed person is low; no outcome-based cut-off was verified.

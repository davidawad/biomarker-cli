# Metanephrines, Fractionated, 24-Hour Urine (`metanephrines-urine-24h`)

Fractionated free metanephrine (and normetanephrine) excreted in a 24-hour urine collection. Elevated excretion supports a diagnosis of pheochromocytoma or paraganglioma; the test is sensitive to interfering drugs and to incomplete collection.

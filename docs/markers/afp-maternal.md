# Alpha-Fetoprotein, Maternal Serum (`afp-maternal`)

Fetal protein that crosses into maternal serum, measured in the second trimester as a multiple of the median (MoM). Raised values flag possible open neural tube defects and abdominal wall defects, and also raise the risk of some pregnancy complications; low values are part of the Down syndrome screen.

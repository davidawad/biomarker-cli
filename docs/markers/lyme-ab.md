# Lyme Disease Antibody (Borrelia burgdorferi) (`lyme-ab`)

Antibodies to Borrelia burgdorferi, the agent of Lyme disease, tested by a two-tier algorithm (ELISA or equivalent first, immunoblot to confirm). Tracked to diagnose Lyme disease after a tick bite with compatible symptoms. Expected result: negative (not detected) in people without past or current infection.

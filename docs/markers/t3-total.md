# Total T3 (Triiodothyronine) (`t3-total`)

Total serum triiodothyronine, bound plus free. Less sensitive than free T3 for thyrotoxicosis and lower in non-thyroidal illness, but useful for T3 toxicosis. Pediatric ranges are reported in nmol/L and kept in that unit.

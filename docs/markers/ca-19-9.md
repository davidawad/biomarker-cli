# Carbohydrate Antigen 19-9 (`ca-19-9`)

Carbohydrate antigen 19-9, a sialylated glycan marker used mainly to monitor pancreatic and biliary cancers. It is not produced in people who lack the Lewis antigen, so a low value does not exclude cancer in them, and it can be raised by benign biliary obstruction and inflammation.

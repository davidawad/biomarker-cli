# Partial Pressure of CO2, Arterial Blood (`pco2`)

Arterial partial pressure of carbon dioxide, the main respiratory measure of ventilation and of respiratory acid-base status. Measured on arterial blood gas analysis. Expected result: at or below the upper limit of normal for the age group.

# CGM glucose coefficient of variation (`cgm-glucose-cv`)

Within-person coefficient of variation (CV, standard deviation divided by mean glucose) of CGM glucose, a measure of glycemic variability. Consensus target for people with diabetes; reference values from healthy participants.

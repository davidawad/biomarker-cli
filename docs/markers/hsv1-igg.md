# Herpes Simplex Virus 1 IgG (`hsv1-igg`)

IgG antibody to herpes simplex virus type 1 (HSV-1), usually an ELISA reported as negative or positive by assay cut-off. Tracked to establish past HSV-1 infection (oral or genital herpes), which is very common and often asymptomatic. Expected result: positive in many adults, which reflects past infection rather than an active outbreak.

# Insulin-like Growth Factor Binding Protein 3 (`igfbp-3`)

Main binding protein carrying IGF-1 in plasma and a GH-dependent marker of GH action. Concentrations rise through childhood and puberty, so age- and sex-specific intervals are needed; assay differences are large.

# Thrombin time (`thrombin-time`)

Clotting time of plasma after a fixed amount of thrombin is added, reflecting conversion of fibrinogen to fibrin. Prolonged values suggest low or dysfunctional fibrinogen, heparin or direct thrombin inhibitor effect, or dysproteinaemia; it is used to detect heparin and fibrinogen abnormalities.

# Lipoprotein(a) Mass (`lpa-mass`)

Lipoprotein(a) reported as mass concentration in mg/dL. Lp(a) is a genetically determined LDL-like particle and a causal cardiovascular risk factor; mass and molar (nmol/L) results are not reliably interconvertible.

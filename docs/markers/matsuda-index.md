# Matsuda Insulin Sensitivity Index (`matsuda-index`)

Whole-body insulin sensitivity index calculated from a 75 g oral glucose tolerance test: 10,000 divided by the square root of (fasting glucose x fasting insulin) x (mean glucose x mean insulin during the OGTT). Higher values mean greater insulin sensitivity. Correlated with the euglycemic clamp (r = 0.73) in the derivation study.

# Calculated Serum Osmolality (`calculated-osmolality`)

Estimated serum osmolality from measured sodium, glucose and urea: 2 x Na + glucose/18 + BUN/2.8 (glucose in mg/dL, BUN in mg/dL). Compared with measured osmolality to compute the osmolal gap, and used to screen for hyperosmolar states and toxic alcohol exposure.

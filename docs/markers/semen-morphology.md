# Sperm Morphology (`semen-morphology`)

Percentage of spermatozoa with normal head, midpiece and tail morphology on a stained semen smear. Tracked with sperm concentration and motility to assess male fertility potential. Expected result: at or above the WHO lower reference limit (4% in both the WHO 2010 and WHO 2021 manuals).

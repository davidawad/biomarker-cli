# Cadmium, whole blood (`cadmium-blood`)

Cadmium concentration in whole blood, reflecting recent exposure from tobacco smoke and food; it rises with smoking. Blood cadmium is a short-term marker, while urinary cadmium better reflects body burden and kidney exposure.

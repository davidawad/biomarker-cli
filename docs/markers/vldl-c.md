# VLDL Cholesterol (`vldl-c`)

Cholesterol carried in very-low-density lipoprotein particles, reported as a measure of atherogenic remnant lipoproteins. Commonly calculated as triglycerides divided by 5 when triglycerides are below 400 mg/dL.

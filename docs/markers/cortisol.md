# Cortisol (AM) (`cortisol`)

Serum cortisol, mainly measured in the morning. Used to screen for adrenal insufficiency (basal and after ACTH stimulation) and, after a dexamethasone suppression test, for Cushing's syndrome. Results depend strongly on the assay (immunoassay vs LC-MS/MS).

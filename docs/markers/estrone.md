# Estrone (`estrone`)

Weak estrogen, the main circulating estrogen after menopause (made mostly by aromatization of androstenedione in fat and other tissues). Measured by LC-MS/MS, since immunoassays are unreliable at low concentrations. Interpret by sex, puberty stage and menopausal status.

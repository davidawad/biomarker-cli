# Potassium (`potassium`)

Serum potassium governs cardiac excitability. Hypokalaemia (<3.5 mmol/L) and hyperkalaemia (>=5.5 mmol/L) both carry arrhythmic risk, and outcome studies in CKD and heart failure show a U-shaped relation with mortality, with the lowest risk around 4-5 mmol/L. Serum values are usually higher than plasma values.

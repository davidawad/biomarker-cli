# Syphilis RPR (Reagin) (`syphilis-rpr`)

Nontreponemal serologic screening test for syphilis (rapid plasma reagin, RPR). Detects reagin antibodies in serum or plasma. Reported as reactive or nonreactive; when reactive, a titer (dilution) is reported and is used to follow treatment response. Expected result: nonreactive.

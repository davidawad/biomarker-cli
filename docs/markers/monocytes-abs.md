# Absolute monocyte count (`monocytes-abs`)

Absolute count of circulating monocytes, precursors of tissue macrophages and dendritic cells. High values (monocytosis) occur with chronic infection such as tuberculosis, inflammation, and myeloid disorders; low values are uncommon and usually drug- or marrow-related.

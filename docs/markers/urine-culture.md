# Urine Culture (`urine-culture`)

Quantitative bacterial culture of a urine specimen to identify a uropathogen and count colony-forming units per mL. The colony count is compared with a threshold to separate infection from contamination. Expected result: no growth or a count below the diagnostic threshold (no significant bacteriuria).

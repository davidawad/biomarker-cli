# Sperm Progressive Motility (`semen-progressive-motility`)

Percentage of spermatozoa moving actively forward (progressive motility) in a semen sample. Tracked with sperm concentration, total motility and morphology to assess male fertility potential. Expected result: at or above the WHO 2010 lower reference limit of 32%.

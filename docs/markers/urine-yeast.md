# Urine Yeast (microscopy) (`urine-yeast`)

Yeast cells seen on microscopic examination of urine sediment, reported as a semi-quantitative grade (none, few, moderate, many per high-power field). Tracked to detect candiduria, which may indicate yeast urinary tract infection or contamination. Expected result: none or few (not detected).

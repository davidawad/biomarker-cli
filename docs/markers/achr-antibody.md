# Acetylcholine Receptor Binding Antibody (`achr-antibody`)

Serum autoantibody to the acetylcholine receptor (AChR-Bi, binding assay), used to support the diagnosis of myasthenia gravis. Expected result in people without myasthenia gravis: negative (not detected). A positive result supports, but does not alone confirm, myasthenia gravis; interpret with clinical and electrodiagnostic findings.

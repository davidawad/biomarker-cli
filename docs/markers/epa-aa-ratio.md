# EPA/arachidonic acid ratio (`epa-aa-ratio`)

Ratio of serum eicosapentaenoic acid to arachidonic acid, an index of the balance between omega-3 and omega-6 eicosanoid precursors. Low values indicate a relative excess of arachidonic acid over EPA.

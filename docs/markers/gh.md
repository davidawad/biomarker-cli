# Growth Hormone (`gh`)

Pituitary hormone secreted in pulses that drives growth and IGF-1 production. Random levels are not diagnostic: acromegaly is assessed by GH suppression during an oral glucose load, and GH deficiency by peak GH after a stimulation test, with cut-offs that depend on the assay, test, BMI and age.

# Hemoglobin S (`hbs`)

Share of total haemoglobin present as sickle haemoglobin (HbS), measured by solubility test, electrophoresis, HPLC or capillary methods. Tracked to screen for sickle cell trait and sickle cell disease. Expected result when no sickle variant is present: not detected.

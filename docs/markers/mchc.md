# Mean Corpuscular Hemoglobin Concentration (`mchc`)

Average concentration of hemoglobin within a red blood cell. Low values suggest hypochromia (for example iron deficiency); high values are seen in spherocytosis and other conditions with abnormal cell hemoglobin content.

# Chromogranin A (`chromogranin-a`)

Neuroendocrine secretory granule protein measured in serum or plasma by immunoassay. Used to screen, diagnose and monitor neuroendocrine neoplasms. Expected result in healthy people: within the reference interval, at or below the upper limit. Levels vary between individuals and assays.

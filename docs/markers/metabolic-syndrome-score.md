# Metabolic Syndrome Score (`metabolic-syndrome-score`)

Count (0 to 5) of the harmonized metabolic syndrome criteria met: raised waist circumference, triglycerides, reduced HDL-C, raised blood pressure and raised fasting glucose. Metabolic syndrome is diagnosed when 3 or more are met.

# Urine Urobilinogen (dipstick) (`urine-urobilinogen`)

Urobilinogen detected in urine by the Ehrlich reaction on a urine dipstick, reported as semi-quantitative grades (Ehrlich units). Tracked as a screen for hepatobiliary disease or hemolysis, and for acute hepatic porphyria. Expected result: normal (low) urobilinogen, not elevated.

# Insulin-like Growth Factor 1 (`igf-1`)

Hepatic mediator of growth hormone action and the main circulating marker of GH secretion. Levels vary strongly with age, puberty and sex, so results must be read against age- and sex-specific intervals; in acromegaly they guide diagnosis and treatment targets.

# IA-2 Autoantibodies (ICA512) (`ia-2-antibodies`)

IgG autoantibodies against insulinoma-associated antigen-2 (IA-2, ICA512), a beta-cell antigen, measured by immunoassay (radiobinding, ELISA or chemiluminescence). Tracked to screen for and classify islet autoimmunity and type 1 diabetes, alongside GADA, IAA and ZnT8A. Expected result: negative (below the assay positivity cut-off).

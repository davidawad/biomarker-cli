# Nucleated red blood cells (NRBC) (`nucleated-rbc`)

Immature red cells with a nucleus that normally stay in bone marrow; they appear in blood with severe hypoxia, marrow stress, haemolysis, infiltration or sepsis. Any count above zero is abnormal in adults.

# Specific IgE, Cow Milk (`ige-cow-milk`)

Serum IgE specific to cow's milk protein, used to support the diagnosis of immediate-type cow's milk allergy. Interpret with history and, where needed, an oral food challenge; a positive level does not by itself prove allergy.

# Fibrinogen (`fibrinogen`)

Clottable plasma protein that forms the fibrin clot, measured functionally (Clauss method). Low levels indicate consumption, dilution or reduced synthesis and raise bleeding risk; levels rise physiologically in pregnancy and are an acute-phase reactant.

# TSH Receptor Antibodies (TRAb/TSI) (`trab`)

Antibodies that stimulate or block the TSH receptor; the main cause of Graves disease and a predictor of fetal and neonatal thyroid dysfunction in pregnancy. Cut-offs are assay-specific and not interchangeable across platforms.

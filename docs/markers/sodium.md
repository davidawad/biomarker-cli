# Sodium (`sodium`)

Serum sodium is the main determinant of plasma osmolality. Hyponatraemia (<135 mmol/L) is the most common electrolyte disorder in clinical practice and is associated with higher mortality; hypernatraemia (>145 mmol/L) is less common.

# Parathyroid Hormone, Intact (`pth-intact`)

Hormone from the parathyroid glands that raises blood calcium and controls phosphate handling. In chronic kidney disease, the KDIGO CKD-MBD guideline gives a target range for PTH in dialysis patients.

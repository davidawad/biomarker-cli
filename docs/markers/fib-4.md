# FIB-4 Index (`fib-4`)

FIB-4 is a blood-based score (age, AST, ALT, platelets) used to stratify risk of advanced liver fibrosis in people with MASLD or other chronic liver disease. Low-risk and high-risk cut-offs are applied to rule out or rule in advanced fibrosis before imaging.

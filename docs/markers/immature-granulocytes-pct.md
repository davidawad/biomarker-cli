# Immature granulocytes (%) (`immature-granulocytes-pct`)

Share of immature granulocytes (myelocytes, metamyelocytes and promyelocytes) among all white blood cells, as counted by an automated analyser. Raised values are seen in bacterial infection, sepsis, inflammation, and marrow stress or myeloid disorders.

# Prostate Specific Antigen (`psa`)

Total prostate-specific antigen, a protein made by the prostate and measured in serum. Levels rise with age, benign prostate enlargement and prostate cancer, so it is used for prostate cancer screening and monitoring with age-specific interpretation.

# Specific IgE, Ragweed (`ige-ragweed`)

Serum IgE antibodies specific to ragweed (Ambrosia) pollen allergens, including the major allergen component Amb a 1. Values at or above the assay positivity threshold indicate sensitisation and must be interpreted with the clinical history.

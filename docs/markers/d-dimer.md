# D-dimer (`d-dimer`)

Fragment released when cross-linked fibrin is broken down by plasmin. A normal result helps exclude venous thromboembolism and pulmonary embolism in patients with low clinical probability; raised values are non-specific and increase with age, pregnancy and inflammation.

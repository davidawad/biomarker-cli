# Factor V Leiden (`factor-v-leiden`)

Genetic variant (F5 c.1601G>A, R506Q) in coagulation factor V that makes it resistant to activated protein C and raises risk of venous thromboembolism. Reported as heterozygous, homozygous or not detected. Tracked for unexplained or recurrent venous clots and before estrogen-containing therapy or pregnancy decisions. Expected result: not detected (negative).

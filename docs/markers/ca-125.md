# Cancer Antigen 125 (`ca-125`)

Cancer antigen 125, a glycoprotein marker used mainly in the evaluation of ovarian cancer. It is also raised by many benign conditions (including menstruation, endometriosis, pregnancy and other pelvic or peritoneal disease), so it is interpreted with clinical context.

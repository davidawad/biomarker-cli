# Mean Arterial Pressure (`mean-arterial-pressure`)

Average arterial pressure over one cardiac cycle, calculated from systolic and diastolic blood pressure. Used to judge organ perfusion, mainly in critical care. Expected result in septic shock: at least 65 mmHg on vasopressors.

# Specific IgE, Dust Mite (D. pteronyssinus) (`ige-dust-mite-d-pteronyssinus`)

Serum IgE specific to Dermatophagoides pteronyssinus (ImmunoCAP). Values below the cut-off below identify patients who are also negative for Der p 1 and Der p 2 specific IgE; this does not by itself diagnose dust mite allergy.

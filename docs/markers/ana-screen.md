# Antinuclear Antibody (ANA) Screen (`ana-screen`)

Screen for antinuclear antibodies by indirect immunofluorescence on HEp-2 cells (or an immunoassay), reported as positive or negative and, when positive, as a serum titer (dilution). Tracked as an autoimmune screening test; a positive result is non-specific and must be interpreted with clinical findings. Expected result: negative. Low-titer positives occur in a minority of healthy people.

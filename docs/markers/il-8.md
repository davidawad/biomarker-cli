# Interleukin-8 (`il-8`)

Chemokine (CXCL8) measured in serum by immunoassay or flow cytometry. Tracked as a marker of neutrophil-recruiting inflammatory activity. Expected result in healthy adults: at or below the sex-specific upper reference limit (values in the single- to low double-digit pg/mL range); the reference values are method-specific.

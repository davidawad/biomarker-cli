# Arachidonic acid (AA) (`arachidonic-acid`)

Arachidonic acid (20:4n-6), the omega-6 precursor of pro-inflammatory eicosanoids, reported as a percent of total fatty acids in the blood lipid fraction measured. Higher values relative to EPA are used as an inflammatory-balance marker.

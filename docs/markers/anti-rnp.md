# Anti-RNP Antibody (`anti-rnp`)

Serum autoantibody to U1 small nuclear ribonucleoprotein (U1-RNP). Associated with mixed connective tissue disease and seen in systemic lupus erythematosus and other connective-tissue disease. Reported as positive or negative (immunoassay or line blot). Expected result: negative.

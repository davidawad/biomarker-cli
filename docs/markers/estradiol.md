# Estradiol (`estradiol`)

Most potent estrogen; reflects ovarian follicular and luteal activity in women and testicular and adrenal sources in men. Interpretation depends strongly on cycle phase, menopausal status, age and assay method (LC-MS/MS preferred at low concentrations).

# H. pylori Stool Antigen (`h-pylori-stool-antigen`)

Detection of Helicobacter pylori antigen in a stool sample, used to test for active H. pylori infection, for example before or after eradication treatment. Reported as positive or negative. Expected result: negative (not detected) in people without H. pylori infection.

# Endomysial IgA Antibodies (`endomysial-iga`)

IgA antibody against endomysium (the smooth-muscle connective tissue around intestinal muscle fibres), detected by indirect immunofluorescence on primate oesophagus or human umbilical cord and reported as positive or negative, sometimes with a titer. Used as a confirmatory test for celiac disease, after a screening tTG-IgA result. Expected result: negative (not detected).

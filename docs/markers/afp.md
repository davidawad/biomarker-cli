# Alpha-Fetoprotein (`afp`)

Alpha-fetoprotein, a fetal serum protein that is normally low in adults. Raised levels are used in hepatocellular carcinoma surveillance and diagnosis, and it can also rise in pregnancy and in germ cell tumours.

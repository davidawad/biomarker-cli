# Coenzyme Q10 (`coq10`)

Total coenzyme Q10 (ubiquinone plus ubiquinol) in plasma or serum, an endogenous lipid-soluble antioxidant and electron carrier in mitochondria. People track it to assess CoQ10 status, for example during supplementation or in cardiovascular, neurological and statin-treated patients. Expected result: not a diagnostic test with an established normal range; in one case-control study, higher concentrations were associated with lower coronary artery disease risk.

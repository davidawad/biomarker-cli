# Glucose Management Indicator (`gmi`)

Glucose Management Indicator (GMI) is an estimate of HbA1c calculated from mean continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) sensor glucose. It gives a CGM-based approximation of average glycaemia, used alongside time-in-range.

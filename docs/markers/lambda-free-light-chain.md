# Lambda Free Light Chains (`lambda-free-light-chain`)

Free lambda light chains are the unbound light-chain fragments of antibodies, made in excess by plasma cells. Raised or abnormal levels are used in screening for monoclonal plasma-cell disorders and light-chain disease.

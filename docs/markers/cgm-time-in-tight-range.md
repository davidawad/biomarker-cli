# CGM Time in Tight Range (70-140 mg/dL) (`cgm-time-in-tight-range`)

Percentage of CGM readings between 70 and 140 mg/dL (3.9-7.8 mmol/L) over a day. A narrower alternative to time in range (70-180 mg/dL), used in diabetes care and research.

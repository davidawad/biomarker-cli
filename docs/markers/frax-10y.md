# FRAX 10-Year Fracture Risk (`frax-10y`)

FRAX 10-year probability of major osteoporotic fracture (MOF: clinical spine, hip, forearm or humerus), computed from clinical risk factors with or without femoral neck BMD. Used to choose between lifestyle advice, BMD testing and drug treatment. Shard encodes the MOF probability only; the hip-fracture probability has separate thresholds that the single-slug schema cannot distinguish.

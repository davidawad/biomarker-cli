# Carboxyhemoglobin (`carboxyhemoglobin`)

Fraction of hemoglobin bound to carbon monoxide, measured by co-oximetry. Used to assess CO exposure and tobacco smoke exposure, and to guide CO poisoning management. Expected result: low single-digit percent in non-smokers; higher in smokers and in CO exposure.

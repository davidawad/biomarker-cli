# Aldosterone (`aldosterone`)

Mineralocorticoid produced by the adrenal zona glomerulosa; regulates sodium and potassium balance. Interpreted together with renin (aldosterone-to-renin ratio) to screen for primary aldosteronism, and by its suppression response to confirmatory tests.

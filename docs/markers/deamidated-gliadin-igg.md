# Deamidated Gliadin IgG (`deamidated-gliadin-igg`)

IgG antibody against synthetic deamidated gliadin peptides (DGP), measured by immunoassay. Used as a serologic test for celiac disease, including in people with IgA deficiency or in whom tTG-IgA is unreliable. Results are assay-specific and reported against a manufacturer or study cut-off. Expected result: negative (below the cut-off).

# Group A Streptococcus Antigen (Rapid) (`strep-a-rapid`)

Rapid immunoassay on a throat swab that detects Group A Streptococcus (Streptococcus pyogenes) carbohydrate antigen, reported as positive or negative within minutes. It is used to diagnose streptococcal pharyngitis. Expected result: negative (not detected).

# Nickel, Serum (`nickel`)

Nickel concentration in serum, reflecting recent nickel exposure from diet, jewellery, coins, dental or metal implants, and occupation. Tracked for exposure assessment and nickel allergy context. Expected result in a non-occupationally exposed person is low.

# Metanephrine, Plasma Free (`metanephrine-plasma-free`)

Free metanephrine, the O-methylated metabolite of epinephrine, measured in plasma. Elevated values support a diagnosis of pheochromocytoma or paraganglioma; plasma free metanephrines are a recommended first-line test.

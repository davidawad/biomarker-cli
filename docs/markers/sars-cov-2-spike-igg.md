# SARS-CoV-2 Spike IgG Antibody (`sars-cov-2-spike-igg`)

Serum IgG antibody against the SARS-CoV-2 spike protein, quantified by immunoassay in BAU/mL (WHO international standard) or vendor units. Tracked after infection or vaccination to gauge humoral response; a higher level is associated with neutralizing antibody activity. Expected result: positive after infection or vaccination; negative means no detectable spike antibody by that assay.

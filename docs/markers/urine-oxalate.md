# Oxalate, Urine (`urine-oxalate`)

Urinary oxalate, the end product of endogenous oxalate metabolism and dietary absorption. Measured to screen for hyperoxaluria, which raises the risk of calcium oxalate kidney stones and oxalate nephropathy, and to diagnose primary hyperoxaluria. Spot results are reported per creatinine; 24-hour results in mmol/d. Expected result: within the age- and sex-specific reference interval; values above the upper limit indicate hyperoxaluria.

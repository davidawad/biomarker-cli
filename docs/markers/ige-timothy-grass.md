# Specific IgE, Timothy Grass (`ige-timothy-grass`)

Serum IgE antibodies specific to Timothy grass (Phleum pratense) pollen extract, measured by ImmunoCAP. Positive results indicate sensitisation to grass pollen and support a diagnosis of grass pollen allergy when they match the clinical history.

# Leptin (`leptin`)

Adipose-derived hormone that signals energy stores to the brain; serum levels track body fat and are higher in women and in people with higher BMI and insulin resistance. Tracked for obesity, metabolic syndrome and energy-balance research. Expected result: within the sex-specific reference interval for a healthy adult (fasting serum).

# Anti-SSB/La Antibody (`anti-ssb-la`)

Autoantibody to the La/SSB ribonucleoprotein antigen, detected by immunoassay and reported as positive or negative. Associated with Sjogren syndrome and systemic lupus erythematosus, usually found together with anti-SSA/Ro. Expected result: negative (not detected).

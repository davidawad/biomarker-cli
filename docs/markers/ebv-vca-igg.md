# Epstein-Barr Virus VCA IgG (`ebv-vca-igg`)

IgG antibody to Epstein-Barr virus viral capsid antigen (VCA). Present during acute EBV infection and persists after it, so it indicates EBV exposure but cannot by itself date the infection; it is interpreted with VCA IgM and EBNA-1 IgG. Expected result: negative in people never exposed to EBV; positive in people with past or current infection.

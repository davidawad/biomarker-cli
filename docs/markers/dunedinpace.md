# DunedinPACE (Pace of Aging) (`dunedinpace`)

DNA methylation estimate of the pace of biological ageing, expressed as biological years gained per chronological year. A value of 1.0 means ageing at the average rate of one biological year per calendar year; higher values indicate faster ageing and higher morbidity and mortality risk.

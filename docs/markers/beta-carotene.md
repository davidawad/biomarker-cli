# Beta-carotene (`beta-carotene`)

Serum beta-carotene, a provitamin A carotenoid from plant foods that is also an antioxidant. Low values reflect low dietary intake or fat malabsorption; high values usually come from high carotenoid intake (harmless carotenodermia) rather than disease.

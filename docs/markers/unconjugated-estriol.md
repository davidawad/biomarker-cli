# Unconjugated Estriol, Maternal Serum (`unconjugated-estriol`)

Fetoplacental estrogen measured in maternal serum in the second trimester, expressed as a multiple of the median (MoM). It is part of the quadruple Down syndrome screen. Low values are associated with Down syndrome and with adverse pregnancy outcomes such as small-for-gestational-age infants and preterm birth.

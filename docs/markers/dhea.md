# DHEA (Dehydroepiandrosterone, unconjugated) (`dhea`)

Unconjugated adrenal androgen precursor, measured by LC-MS/MS. Levels fall steeply with age, and the sulfated form DHEA-S is a separate marker. Interpret against age- and sex-matched intervals.

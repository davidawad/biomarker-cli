# Platelet-to-Lymphocyte Ratio (`plr`)

Platelet-to-lymphocyte ratio (PLR) is the platelet count divided by the lymphocyte count from a complete blood count, a computed inflammation index. It is tracked as a low-cost systemic inflammation marker and is studied as a prognostic index in cancer and cardiovascular disease. No single clinical cut-off is established. Expected result in healthy people: within the sex- and age-specific reference interval, which varies by study and analyser.

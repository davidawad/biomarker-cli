# Free PSA (`psa-free`)

Free PSA, reported as the percentage of free to total PSA, used with total PSA to assess prostate cancer risk and biopsy need. Expected result: a percentage where lower values carry higher risk; a percentage cut-off applies to men with total PSA in the cited range.

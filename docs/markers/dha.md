# Docosahexaenoic acid (DHA) (`dha`)

Docosahexaenoic acid (22:6n-3), the main long-chain omega-3 fatty acid in neural and retinal membranes, reported as a percent of total fatty acids in the blood lipid fraction measured. Low values indicate low marine omega-3 intake or status.

# CYFRA 21-1 (Cytokeratin 19 Fragment) (`cyfra-21-1`)

Cytokeratin 19 fragment measured in serum by immunoassay. Tracked as a tumour marker, mainly for non-small cell lung cancer, and for monitoring. Expected result in healthy people: at or below the reference upper limit.

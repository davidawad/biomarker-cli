# Urine Calcium (`urine-calcium`)

Calcium excreted in urine, usually collected over 24 hours to assess calcium stone risk (hypercalciuria), calcium balance and bone metabolism. Expected result: below the sex-specific hypercalciuria cut-offs in the ranges below. Ranges are copied in the units the sources state; the mmol/24h cut-offs are a companion unit.

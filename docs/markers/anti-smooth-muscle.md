# Anti-Smooth Muscle Antibody (`anti-smooth-muscle`)

Autoantibody to smooth muscle and cytoskeletal antigens (mainly filamentous actin), detected by immunofluorescence and reported as a titer (dilution). Used with ANA and other liver autoantibodies in the workup of suspected type 1 autoimmune hepatitis. Expected result in people without autoimmune hepatitis: not detected, or a titer at or below 1:40.

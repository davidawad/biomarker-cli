# Valine (`valine`)

Valine is a branched-chain essential amino acid measured in serum or plasma by LC-MS/MS or HPLC. It is tracked as a nutritional and metabolic marker, and as part of amino acid profiles in inborn errors of metabolism. Expected result in healthy people: within the age-specific reference interval.

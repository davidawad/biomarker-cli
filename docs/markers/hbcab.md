# Hepatitis B Core Antibody, Total (`hbcab`)

Antibody (IgG and IgM) to the hepatitis B core antigen, reported as reactive or non-reactive, and as a signal-to-cutoff index by some assays. A reactive result shows past or current exposure to hepatitis B virus; it does not by itself separate resolved infection from chronic infection, which needs HBsAg and IgM anti-HBc testing. Expected result: non-reactive (negative) in people never exposed to hepatitis B. Hepatitis B vaccination alone does not produce anti-HBc.

# Iron deficiency and iron deficiency anaemia (`iron-anemia`)

Confirming iron deficiency, characterising the anaemia and screening for cause, per the British Society of Gastroenterology guideline.

## Core

| Marker | Reason | Citations |
|---|---|---|
| [Ferritin](../markers/ferritin.md) | Single most useful marker of iron deficiency anaemia; below 15 ug/L means absent stores. | [British Society of Gastroenterology guidelines for the management of iron deficiency anaemia in adults.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34497146/), Recommendation 4 |
| [Transferrin Saturation](../markers/transferrin-saturation.md) | Helpful when a false-normal ferritin is suspected (inflammation); below 20% indicates iron restriction. | [British Society of Gastroenterology guidelines for the management of iron deficiency anaemia in adults.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34497146/), Recommendation 4 |
| [Hemoglobin](../markers/hemoglobin.md) | Defines anaemia, predicts GI cancer risk and tracks response to iron (rise >=10 g/L in 2 weeks). | [British Society of Gastroenterology guidelines for the management of iron deficiency anaemia in adults.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34497146/), Recommendation 9 |
| [Mean Corpuscular Volume](../markers/mcv.md) | Microcytosis accompanies iron deficiency and independently predicts GI cancer risk. | [British Society of Gastroenterology guidelines for the management of iron deficiency anaemia in adults.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34497146/), Recommendation 9 |
